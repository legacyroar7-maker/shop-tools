    Will your title fit?

    Type or paste a product title. The counts update as you type. Words past the limit are cut off or not accepted, so put the most important words first.

    Limits used: Etsy title 140 characters and eBay title 80 characters (the platform will not accept more). Shopify SEO title about 60 and meta description about 155 characters (what Google usually shows in full; longer text gets cut). Platforms change their rules, so check the help pages of yours.

      Check your 13 Etsy tags

      Paste your tags, one per line or with commas between them. Add your title too, so the tool can spot tags that only repeat it. The results update as you type.

      Rules used, from Etsy's help pages: up to 13 tags, up to 20 characters each. Use letters, numbers and spaces. An apostrophe (') or hyphen (-) is fine inside a tag but not as the first character. "Copy tags for Etsy" copies up to 13 tags that pass these rules, with extra spaces and repeats removed, separated by commas, ready to paste into the Tags box. Platforms change their rules, so check Etsy's help pages too.

        Write alt text for your product photos

        Alt text tells shoppers who use a screen reader what is in each photo. It also helps search engines and AI tools understand your images. Fill in the product once, tick the photos you have, and get short alt text for each one. You can edit every line.

          Where to paste it

          Shopify: Products, open the product, click the photo, add alt text, save.
          Etsy: Shop Manager, Listings, open the listing, click the photo, fill in "Alt text", press Apply.

          Shopify takes up to 512 characters and suggests 125 or fewer. Etsy takes up to about 250. This tool uses 125 for both, so the text is quick to hear. Platforms change their rules, so check their help pages too.
